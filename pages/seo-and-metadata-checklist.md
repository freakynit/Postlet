Before publishing a post, check these fields.

## Required

- `title`
- `description`
- `date`
- `tags`

## Recommended

- `canonical`
- `keywords`
- `ogImage`

## Optional control flags

- `published: false` for drafts
- `noindex: true` for utility pages
