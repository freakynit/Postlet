This demo is intentionally small and practical.

## Goals

- Show clean frontmatter conventions
- Demonstrate tags and metadata for SEO output
- Provide starter posts you can keep, edit, or delete

## How to make it yours

1. Update `config.json` with your site URL, title, and author.
2. Replace these pages with your own writing.
3. Pick a theme from `themes/`.
4. Add your own plugins in `plugins/` or `src/plugins/`.
